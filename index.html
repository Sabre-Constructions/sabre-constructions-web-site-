Before you ring

A few more questions people ask.

Do we need our own plans?

No. Start from one of six Sabre designs and change what you like, or bring your own architect's drawings. Sabre builds both ways.

Where do you build?

Capalaba is home. Wynnum, Manly, Lota, Wakerley, Birkdale, Wellington Point, Cleveland and across the Bayside and Redlands.

Do you do renovations and raises, or only new homes?

Both. New homes, knockdown rebuilds, raises, renovations and extensions. Reviews on the testimonials page cover all of them.
