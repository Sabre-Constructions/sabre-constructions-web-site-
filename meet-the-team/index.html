Who you get

The people
on your build.

Six of them. Two founded the company in 1990, one is their son, and the other three have been here long enough to be named in Google reviews. There's no sales team. The person who quotes your house is the person who builds it.

Stewart Brain

Owner and builder

On the tools at seventeen. Founded Sabre with Cathy in 1990 and has more than thirty years as a carpenter and builder. He's the one who walks your block. Stewart competed in judo for Australia at the 1988 Seoul Olympics and took bronze at the 1986 Commonwealth Games, and later coached athletes to the 2012 and 2016 Games.

Cathy Brain

Owner and business manager

The backbone of the company. Cathy runs accounts, sales and employment. She represented Australia in judo at three Olympic Games, 1988, 1992 and 1996, and won bronze at the 1990 Auckland Commonwealth Games. She and Stewart founded Ohori Judo Club together.

Nathan Brain

Carpenter

The middle Brain child and the second generation on the tools. Nathan came through his carpentry apprenticeship at Sabre and is on site through the build. Customers name him in their reviews.

Frank Doorly

Site supervisor

Seventeen years at Sabre. Frank runs the day-to-day of each build. One review puts it simply: he kept us informed all the way through.

Celene Van Bladeren

Accounts and operations

Client liaison, suppliers, scheduling and accounts. Celene is the person you'll speak to most between site visits.

Jacob Campbell

Estimator

The youngest on the team. Jacob works with Stewart and with you to keep the build lined up with what you need and what you can spend, and prices changes before they're made.
